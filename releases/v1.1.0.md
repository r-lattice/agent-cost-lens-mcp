# Agent Cost Lens v1.1.0 — honest numbers for Max/Pro plans

*2026-07-20*

If you're on a Claude Max or Pro plan, every cost tool lies to you a little:
it prices your tokens at API list rates and calls that "spend." You didn't
spend it — your plan is flat-fee. And "recoverable savings" aren't dollars for
you either; they're usage-limit headroom.

**v1.1.0 makes the lens say what's actually true for subscription users.**

## What's new

Tell the lens your plan once:

```json
// ~/.config/agent-cost-lens/plan.json
{"name": "Max 20x", "monthly_cost": 200.0}
```

and every figure is reworded for flat-fee reality:

```
29 sessions · $164.36 API-equivalent (Max 20x: $0 marginal, flat $200/mo)
· equiv run-rate ≈ $2,465/mo = 12.3× plan price · 97% cache-hit
· caching saved $862.98 of headroom (84%) · recoverable $0.03 of headroom
```

- **"Spent" becomes "API-equivalent"** — the value you consumed at list
  prices, explicitly not money that left your account.
- **Savings become "usage-limit headroom"** — what caching (and fixes) buy you
  on a capped plan is more usage before hitting limits, and the lens says so.
- **Equivalent run-rate vs. plan price** — the number that actually matters on
  a subscription: how many times over your flat fee is paying for itself.
  (12× is a very good reason to stay on Max.)

## The contracts

- **No plan configured → nothing changes.** Output is byte-identical to
  v1.0.0, locked by test. Plan-awareness is opt-in and fails dark.
- **The wire carries two values** — plan name and monthly price. Nothing else
  from your config; the privacy surface is unchanged.
- **The landing page's claim is unit-test-enforced**: the build fails if
  plan-aware wording ever drifts into claiming dollar savings for flat-fee
  users.
